# glmnet-coxsig

## When to use / when not
Expression signature development or independent validation, never a clinical-only model. One
right-censored event per biological subject. No post-selection coefficient p-values, causal claims,
competing risks, recurrent events, interactions or automatic gene selection before cross-validation.

## Input requirements
Mandatory --from-run expression_matrix and exact clinical subject IDs, explicit 0/1 events and
positive times. Gene IDs/order, normalized expression scale and time units must be declared.
Gene-only covariates none is explicit. Candidate/display-only/batch-corrected expression is refused.
At most 100 prespecified genes, 5000 subjects, 20 CV folds and ten ROC horizons; no downsampling.
Training requires supplied unit,fold CSV, each holdout at least five subjects and two events,
standardization, path extent/grid, objective, grouped-risk-set choice, convergence settings,
penalty-selection rule and risk-cutoff rule. Breslow ties are explicit across glmnet versions. Training requires glmnet >=5.0 and uses its control API.

## Run
The canonical fixture command is in tests/acceptance.json. --mode train requires training flags;
--mode validate requires --signature-run and refuses every training/tuning/cutpoint flag.
Validation declares the same gene order, preprocessing scale and time units. Use the same
prespecified study contract covering both cohorts. Globally unique subject IDs cannot overlap
training; a matching label is treated conservatively as overlap, not silently renamed.
All runs require --development-mode while candidate status persists.

## What gets produced
Typed cox_signature RDS with named original-scale coefficients, gene order, preprocessing declaration,
selected lambda, frozen training risk cutoff and hashed training identities. Tables include scores,
coefficients, CV curve, coefficient paths, IPCW AUC/ROC and descriptive KM/risk counts.
Figures use shared builders. Full input/fold/signature hashes, QC, method text and replay spec retained.
External validation writes the unchanged signature object; no coefficient or cutoff is refit.

## Reading the results
Training ROC is apparent performance, even when CV selected lambda. CV penalty selection does not
make training AUC external validation. No AUC CI or post-selection coefficient p is fabricated.
Returned glmnet coefficients already undo its standardization; validation multiplies original-scale
expression by those coefficients without restandardizing to the validation cohort.
The supplied study preprocessing must make cohorts comparable; a matching scale label alone is
not experimental proof. Marginal Kaplan-Meier IPCW assumes independent censoring; no covariate
censoring model is inferred. KM score groups are descriptive and have no logrank test.
A null/constant score remains valid; when its frozen split is uninformative, KM is omitted, never recut.

## When a QC rule fires
CXS-01 blocks candidate publication and clinical application. CXS-02 labels apparent training ROC.
CXS-03 explains omitted KM when a frozen group has fewer than two subjects. Invalid folds,
nonconvergence, leakage, unsupported horizons or mismatched signature are typed refusals.

## Known limitations
Gene-only LASSO alpha=1, right censoring and marginal IPCW. No nested-CV performance estimator,
absolute event-risk prediction, AUC uncertainty, categorical clinical model, or automated threshold
optimization. Owner scientific/visual review and locked-image exec remain pending.
