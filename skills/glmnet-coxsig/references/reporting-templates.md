# Reporting structure

State dataset/version, independent n/events, expression preprocessing, all candidate genes, source
planning assumptions/citation, time units and horizon, CV/penalty and candidate status. Report actual
required Riley n even when unmet. Distinguish apparent, optimism, corrected and external performance.
Report requested/used/failed bootstrap counts and the source percentile construction limitation.
Report the source-native diagnostic slope and marginal Wald interval beside calibration, plus O/E.
Training curve is apparent, not corrected. Every plotted bin needs n, observed KM risk/log-log CI,
frozen predicted mean risk and at-risk count. Original/displayed/omitted subject counts must reconcile.
External inference consumes the same coefficient/baseline workup hash and may not retune settings.

Real owner evidence may contain only permitted aggregate outputs and exact data/code/table/figure
bindings. Never upload clinical rows, individual scores, signature objects containing row-linked
artifacts, or source patient examples. Synthetic software fixtures cannot satisfy owner anchors.
