# Troubleshooting

OS_HANDOFF_MISMATCH: use the exact accepted expression producer; no handcrafted acceptance state.
OS_BATCH_CORRECTED_INPUT / OS_DISPLAY_ONLY_INPUT: obtain the eligible upstream input, never relabel.
OS_INVALID_DESIGN: review exact IDs, positive follow-up, nominated-event consistency, prespecified
genes/covariates and period event support. Do not jitter, drop subjects or change methods to pass.
OS_MISSING_PARAMETER: ask for all conditional choices at once; no automatic non-PH remedy or cuts.
OS_INVALID_PARAMETER: use sorted unique declared cuts/times, explicit source-native piecewise
policy and Aalen-Johansen point-only CIF. Optimized CIF splits and 1-KM incidence are refused.
OS_INVALID_INPUT: status must be finite integer0..k with consecutive event codes and distinct
binary-event column. No implicit recoding. Missing cause data is a real failure.
OS_INVALID_RESULT: preserve invalid/nonfinite diagnostics, AJ probabilities or split failures;
do not manufacture estimates. Native missing dependencies remain explicit blockers.

Cumulative-incidence restyling is not implemented. Obtain a fresh allowed native render from the
stored aggregate tables when the dedicated renderer path becomes available; do not claim the
existing five-type restyle service can already replay the new type.
