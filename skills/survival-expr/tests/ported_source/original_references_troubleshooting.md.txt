# Troubleshooting

OS_HANDOFF_MISMATCH requires the exact unmodified expression producer. OS_INVALID_DESIGN
requires inspecting identities, event counts, model rank and declared genes. Do not jitter constant
expression or drop subjects to force a model fit. OS_BATCH_CORRECTED_INPUT requires original
uncorrected expression and explicit covariate design, not relabelling a corrected artifact.
