# Troubleshooting

OS_CELL_MISMATCH: verify unique IDs, subset membership and disjoint query/reference cells. OS_MISSING_REDUCTION: provide the correct existing graph or representation. OS_DISPLAY_ONLY_INPUT: return to the original uncurated cohort for inference. OS_TOO_MANY_CATEGORIES: supply every category colour. OS_RESULT_INCONSISTENT: reference coordinates changed or cannot be represented exactly as float32. Rebuild the reference in float32 first.
